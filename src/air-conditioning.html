Air Cooled Units

Air Cooled Marine Air Conditioning

Our marine air cooled units are designed to operate in the most extreme onboard conditions. All units are produced as split systems comprising an internal evaporating section and external condensing section. The VK and FP range can also be supplied as close coupled self-contained units. Standard options include SS316 casings, ATEX rated fans and equipment, anti-condensation heaters and more.

Hazardous Area

ATEX Rated Hazardous Area Air Conditioning

For applications in classified hazardous areas — gas tankers, FPSOs, chemical carriers, pump rooms and offshore platforms — ANP supplies the FP range with full ATEX certification for the external condensing section. An A60 fire resistant division separates the internal and external sections.

ATEX · Hazardous Area
FP Hazardous Area Air Cooled Unit
3.5 kW · 5.25 kW · 7.0 kW
The FP unit is specifically designed where the external condensing section is located in or adjacent to a hazardous area (Zone 1 or Zone 2). The external section is fully ATEX certified while the internal evaporator section is suitable for non-hazardous installation.
