# Case Studies

## North Sea FPSO – Ballast & Firewater System Overhaul

**Client**: Major International Oil & Gas Operator  
**Asset**: North Sea FPSO  

**Problem**: Chronic ballast pump failures and pipeline corrosion causing frequent downtime and class non-conformities.

**Solution**: Full ballast system integrity campaign including pump refurbishment, ultrasonic testing, and pipeline repairs during a planned shutdown.

**Results**:
- Reduced pump failures by **62%** over 12 months
- Achieved class approval with **zero conditions**
- Saved approximately **185 hours** of unplanned downtime per year

---

## LNG Carrier – Cargo Refrigeration Upgrade

**Client**: Leading LNG Shipping Company  
**Asset**: 170,000 m³ LNG Carrier  

**Problem**: High boil-off rates and inefficient refrigeration systems.

**Solution**: Complete overhaul of cargo refrigeration plant with modern compressors and monitoring systems during dry-docking.

**Results**:
- Reduced daily boil-off rate by **18%**
- Annual fuel savings of approximately **$380,000**
- Full compliance with latest IMO and IGC Code requirements

---

## Platform Supply Vessel (PSV) – HVAC Retrofit

**Client**: Tier-1 Offshore Support Contractor  
**Asset**: UK Continental Shelf PSV  

**Problem**: Failing HVAC system causing poor crew comfort in harsh North Sea conditions.

**Solution**: Full HVAC retrofit including new air handling units and variable frequency drives while vessel remained operational.

**Results**:
- Improved temperature stability by **45%**
- Reduced crew complaints by **85%**
- Achieved **100% MLC 2006** compliance

## VLCC Crude Tanker – Compressed Air System Overhaul

**Client**: Global Shipping Major  
**Asset**: Very Large Crude Carrier  

**Problem**: Frequent compressed air breakdowns affecting cargo and safety systems.

**Solution**: Complete compressed air system reliability project including compressor replacement and predictive sensors.

**Results**:
- Reduced downtime by **78%**
- Zero lost-time incidents related to air systems in the following year
- Extended major overhaul interval by 2 years

## Offshore Wind Installation Vessel – Emergency Response

**Client**: Leading Wind Farm Developer  
**Asset**: Jack-up Wind Turbine Installation Vessel  

**Problem**: Critical refrigeration failure during wind farm campaign threatening crew welfare.

**Solution**: 24/7 emergency mobilization, rapid repair and upgrade while vessel stayed on location.

**Results**:
- Mobilized within **18 hours**
- Prevented **14 days** of potential delay
- Zero lost production time
