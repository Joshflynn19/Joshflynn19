Planned Maintenance

Dry Dock & Port Call Support
Built Around Your Schedule.

Ship superintendents and technical managers work to fixed windows. We do too. ANP Marine deploys engineers to drydocks and port calls with pre-agreed work scopes, coordinated with your classification surveyor, and timed to the hour — so your vessel leaves on schedule, fully certified.

01
Work Pack Review & Planning

We receive your planned maintenance list, defect reports and class requirements — typically 4–6 weeks ahead of drydock or port call. We produce a detailed work scope, labour plan and spares list aligned to your dockyard schedule.

02
Class Surveyor Coordination

Our engineers liaise directly with Lloyd's Register, DNV GL, Bureau Veritas and other class societies. We prepare the technical documentation, witness test schedules and certification packages required for survey sign-off during the drydock window.

03
Multi-System Execution & Handover

We manage concurrent work packages across HVAC, refrigeration, compressed air, pumps and valves — with daily progress reporting to the superintendent. Full service records, test certificates and class documentation are handed over before departure.
